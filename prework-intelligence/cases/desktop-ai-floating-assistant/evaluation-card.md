## Case Type

## Why This Case Exists

This case tests whether the workflow can distinguish ordinary AI sidebars, desktop assistants, and true always-available context-aware assistants.

## Must Discover

- Desktop AI entry points such as global shortcuts, companion windows, mini chat, and floating assistants.
- Whether tools can read screen, selected text, files, or active-window context.
- Platform support, privacy, permission, API-key, and local-model constraints.
- Whether a single mature tool satisfies the full Windows-oriented use case.

## Must Not Misjudge

- Do not treat a browser sidebar as a global desktop assistant.
- Do not treat a chat overlay as context-aware screen access.
- Do not ignore privacy, permissions, pricing, and platform coverage.
